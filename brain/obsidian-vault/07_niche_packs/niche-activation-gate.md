# Niche activation gate

A niche pack cannot be activated because it sounds plausible. It requires:

- sourced vocabulary, workflows, operating hours, and customer journey;
- a completed sensitive-topic and escalation review;
- approved claims and capability mapping;
- a qualification rubric that does not infer facts;
- niche-specific consent/jurisdiction/recording analysis;
- human specialist training and availability;
- regression cases and adversarial cases;
- pilot cohort, success thresholds, and stop thresholds; and
- owner, version, expiry, and rollback release.

Start with one narrow niche and a small consented pilot. Do not use hospitals or other sensitive settings until the legal, privacy, procurement, security, and specialist-capacity requirements are separately approved.
