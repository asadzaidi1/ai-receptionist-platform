# Roles and responsibilities

| Role | Owns | Must not do |
|---|---|---|
| Business owner | offer truth, approvals, priorities | approve unverified legal claims |
| Compliance reviewer | rules, consent, suppression, audits | silently change product facts |
| Knowledge owner | note accuracy and release process | publish without evidence |
| Campaign operator | lists, pacing, configuration | bypass dial gate |
| AI calling agent | approved conversation and logging | improvise material facts |
| Senior specialist | custom scope, pricing, closing | assume missing context |
| QA reviewer | scoring and failure analysis | edit evidence retroactively |
| Technical owner | integrations, uptime, incidents | hide failures |
