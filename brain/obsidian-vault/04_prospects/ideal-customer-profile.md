# Ideal customer profile

The initial ICP is intentionally incomplete until pilot data exists.

## Candidate profile

A legitimate service business that receives customer calls, has meaningful periods when staff are busy or unavailable, and can benefit from better inquiry capture or appointment handling.

## Required discovery signals

- owner or manager can discuss phone operations;
- missed, busy-period, or after-hours calls are a real problem;
- the business has an identifiable booking or callback process;
- a decision-maker can evaluate a demo; and
- the business is willing to use an approved phone/calendar workflow.

## Exclusions until approved

- emergency or crisis services;
- situations requiring medical, legal, financial, or other professional judgment;
- organizations with no authorized contact route;
- campaigns with unresolved consent or jurisdiction; and
- niches without an approved module.
