# Objection library

## “We already have a receptionist.”

> “That makes sense. This is usually positioned as overflow or after-hours support, not as a judgment on your current staff. When your team is tied up or closed, what happens to the caller?”

## “We do not need it.”

> “Understood. Before I let you go, is that because calls are already covered after hours, or because the problem is not significant?”

Ask one light check at most. Accept a no.

## “Send information.”

> “I can note that. What would you want the information to answer, and is there a good time for a specialist to follow up?”

Do not treat an information request as qualified unless the definition’s agreed next step is met.

## “Is this a human?”

> “I’m an AI assistant calling on behalf of {company name}. I can answer the general questions I’m approved for, and a specialist can handle anything more specific.”
