# Product overview: AI receptionist

## What is the product?

The AI receptionist is a configurable phone assistant intended to help a business handle inquiries, capture details, and support appointment or callback workflows when staff are busy or unavailable.

## What must be confirmed before saying it?

The live capabilities register must confirm supported phone systems, calendars, languages, hours, transfer behavior, integrations, setup timeline, data handling, and limits. If a capability is not marked live, say that a specialist must confirm it.

## Positioning

Describe the service as overflow and after-hours coverage. Do not promise that it replaces every front-desk task or eliminates every missed call.

## Draft answer

> “The receptionist can answer approved inquiries, collect the details your team needs, and help with the next step when staff are busy or closed. A specialist can confirm exactly how it would fit your current phone and booking process.”
