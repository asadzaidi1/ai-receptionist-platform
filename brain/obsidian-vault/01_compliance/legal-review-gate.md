# Counsel review gate

A campaign cannot move to `approved` until counsel has reviewed the exact facts, not a generic business description.

## Review packet

- legal seller and brands;
- target states and how recipient location is determined;
- number types and classification vendor;
- lead source and exact consent language/evidence;
- AI/artificial voice and dialing method;
- caller ID and callback number;
- opt-out and DNC process;
- calling windows and retries;
- recording/transcription/monitoring;
- data flows, vendors, subprocessors, retention, deletion, and security;
- scripts, claims, price statements, handoff behavior, and voicemail;
- complaint/incident response; and
- counsel name, opinion scope, date, assumptions, and expiry/review date.

A legal research URL is not a legal opinion. The FCC AI-voice ruling, FTC TSR guidance, and state examples are inputs to the review, not a substitute for campaign-specific advice.
