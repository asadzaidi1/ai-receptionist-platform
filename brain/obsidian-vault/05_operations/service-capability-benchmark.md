# Service capability benchmark

Public benchmark source: `https://hirebexa.ai/`.

This note records visible service themes for product planning only. It does not verify vendor claims and must not copy proprietary code, prompts, customer data, branding, or unverified performance/ROI claims into our offer.

## Capability themes to support

- 24/7 front-desk intake and qualification;
- specialized bounded agents;
- scheduling and clear next steps;
- verified human handoffs;
- searchable call records and restricted transcript/recording references;
- CRM/dispatch synchronization;
- operational dashboards;
- vertical-specific service packs; and
- human-assisted QA and continuous calibration.

## Non-negotiable controls

All service capabilities remain subject to [[consent-gate]], [[dnc-and-suppression-operations]], [[runtime-contract]], [[call-data-management-controller]], and [[06_performance/release-gate]].

No vendor marketing claim becomes an approved claim without evidence and review. No dashboard, prompt, or agent may bypass the dispatch gate, suppression, audit, state machine, or handoff acceptance requirement.

## Product direction

Build a governed AI receptionist and operations layer: capture the inquiry, qualify with evidence, create the correct next step, synchronize the CRM, and keep the handoff auditable.
