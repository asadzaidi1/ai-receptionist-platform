# Qualified lead definition

A lead is **qualified** only when all three are true:

1. **Right person.** You spoke with the owner, or with a manager the owner named as the decision-maker.
2. **Real problem.** They told you, in their own words, that calls go unanswered at some point (after hours, at lunch, on weekends, when the team is tied up) and that it costs them customers.
3. **An agreed next step.** A demo at a specific day and time, or a live conversation with a senior representative right now.

Anything less is not qualified. Log it honestly as one of:

- **Callback:** interested but not now, or the owner wasn't available. Needs a callback time and a number.
- **Not for us:** they said no, it's the wrong business, or there's no need. Note the reason in their words.
- **Stop calling:** they asked not to be called again. Honor it at once and log it as do-not-call.

Write the problem in the owner's own words in the call sheet. The senior representative reads it before picking up.

Never mark a lead qualified to look good. A wrong "qualified" wastes the senior representative's time and the owner's trust.
