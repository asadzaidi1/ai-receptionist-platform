# Governance vendor register

Maintain one authoritative vendor register with legal entity, service, data categories, processing purpose, region, subprocessors, security evidence, retention/deletion, incident contact, SLA, rate limits, export/exit path, contract status, and owner.

No vendor may be used for live calls, recordings, transcripts, CRM data, consent evidence, or model evaluation until the vendor-risk note and campaign approval reference it.
