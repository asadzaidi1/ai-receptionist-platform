# KPIs and unit economics

## Funnel metrics

- eligible leads;
- attempted calls;
- connection rate;
- decision-maker connection rate;
- qualified conversation rate;
- demo-booking rate;
- human-handoff rate;
- show rate; and
- transfer-to-close rate.

## Trust and reliability metrics

- opt-out rate;
- complaint rate;
- unsupported-claim rate;
- correct-disposition rate;
- required-field completeness;
- successful handoff rate;
- consent-gate block rate;
- latency and error rate; and
- campaign pause incidents.

## Unit economics

Track cost per eligible lead, cost per connected call, cost per qualified transfer, cost per demo shown, and cost per closed customer. Do not optimize for calls made or minutes talked if quality, trust, or compliance worsens. Thresholds are TODO until pilot data exists.
