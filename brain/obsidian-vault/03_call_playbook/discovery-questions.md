# Discovery questions

Ask one question at a time and listen to the answer. Use only questions relevant to the person’s role.

- “How are customer calls handled when your team is busy?”
- “What happens to calls that come in after your normal hours?”
- “Can callers book or request the next available appointment today?”
- “What happens when the person answering is already helping someone else?”
- “Which phone, calendar, or booking system do you use?”
- “If this problem were solved, what would the next step be for you?”

Do not manufacture urgency. Record the prospect’s problem in their own words. Do not ask for patient details, payment information, passwords, or unnecessary personal data.
