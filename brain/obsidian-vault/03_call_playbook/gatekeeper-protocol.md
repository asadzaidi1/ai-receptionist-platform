# Gatekeeper protocol

Treat the first person who answers with respect; do not imply that their role is an obstacle.

- Identify the company and purpose honestly.
- Ask whether the owner or manager handles phone and appointment decisions.
- If unavailable, ask for the best approved callback route and time.
- Do not pressure the staff member to disclose private information.
- Do not pretend to be a customer, vendor, existing partner, or returning caller.

Suggested wording:

> “I’m an AI assistant calling on behalf of {company name} about overflow and after-hours call handling. I may have reached the wrong person—who normally looks after that, and is there an appropriate time to reach them?”
