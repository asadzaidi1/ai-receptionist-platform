# Consent evidence standard

A consent record is sufficient for a campaign only when the evidence proves the relevant person/number, seller, purpose, channel, AI-voice use where required, date/time, collection flow, exact wording/version, affirmative action, and scope.

Do not treat a business directory, purchased list, public website, inbound email signature, or employee statement as a substitute for the required consent evidence. Keep a cryptographic hash and controlled evidence reference in the ledger; keep raw proof in the secured evidence store.

When evidence is incomplete or mismatched, the result is `DO_NOT_DIAL`. Counsel may approve a documented exception only for the exact campaign and facts.
