# Privacy and security answers

This note is intentionally conservative until the company’s security facts are documented.

## Draft answer

> “I do not want to guess about your security or privacy requirements. Our specialist can explain the current data flows, vendors, retention, access controls, and any contractual terms.”

Do not say “secure,” “fully compliant,” “HIPAA compliant,” “encrypted everywhere,” or similar claims unless the exact claim is approved in `approved-claims.md` with evidence. Do not collect patient, payment, password, or sensitive personal information during prospecting.
