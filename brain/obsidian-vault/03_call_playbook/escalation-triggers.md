# Escalation triggers

Transfer or schedule a human specialist when:

- the prospect asks for a human;
- pricing, contract, refund, discount, or custom terms are requested;
- security, privacy, legal, healthcare, or regulatory questions arise;
- the prospect asks about an unsupported integration or workflow;
- the agent is uncertain about a material fact;
- the prospect makes a complaint or alleges harm;
- the prospect is ready to buy or requests a proposal; or
- the transfer destination is available and the prospect agrees.

If no specialist is available, book a clear callback rather than creating dead air. Never promise a transfer that has not been checked.
