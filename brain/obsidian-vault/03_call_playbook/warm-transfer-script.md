# Warm transfer script

Before transferring, ask permission and confirm that a specialist is available.

> “You’ve explained that {problem in the owner’s words}. I can connect you with a specialist who can answer the setup and pricing questions. Would you like me to make that introduction now?”

The handoff packet must contain business name, contact role, problem, current setup, interest level, questions, objection, promised next step, consent/compliance flags, and what has already been said. If the specialist is unavailable, book a callback instead.
