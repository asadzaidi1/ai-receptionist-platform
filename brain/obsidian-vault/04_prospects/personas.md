# Prospect personas

## Owner

Cares about missed opportunities, cost, control, reputation, and whether implementation will create work. Ask about outcomes and decision timing.

## Manager

Cares about staffing, workflows, coverage, and operational reliability. Ask what happens during busy periods and who approves changes.

## Receptionist or staff member

Cares about workload, customer experience, and whether the service is a threat to their role. Speak respectfully and position the product as support and overflow coverage.

## Gatekeeper

Cares about time and legitimacy. Be transparent, concise, and ask for the proper contact route without pressure.

These are conversation aids, not permissions to infer personal characteristics or manipulate a person.
