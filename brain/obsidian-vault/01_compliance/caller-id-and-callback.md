# Caller ID and callback identity

Use an accurate caller ID and a real callback number that reaches a monitored route. Do not spoof, rotate identities to evade suppression, or use an uncallable number.

At the start of an allowed call, state the responsible seller/business and the actual commercial purpose. The AI identity disclosure is an internal trust requirement and may also be required by applicable jurisdiction-specific law; counsel must confirm the final wording.

The callback route must be tested, staffed, and mapped to the campaign. If the number is unavailable or the identity is unresolved, block the campaign.
