# Approved claims

Only claims listed here may be used in a sales call. Every claim needs a source, owner, review date, scope, and evidence.

## Claim rules

- Do not promise revenue, booking volume, cost savings, or a specific conversion result.
- Do not claim legal, regulatory, security, healthcare, or industry certification without exact evidence and approval.
- Do not imply a feature exists because a vendor offers it.
- When evidence is missing, say that a specialist will confirm it and log a knowledge gap.
