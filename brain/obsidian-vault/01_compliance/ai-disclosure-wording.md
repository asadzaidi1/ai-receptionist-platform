# AI disclosure wording

This note is a draft. The company name, agent persona, recording notice, and final disclosure require review before live use.

## Required information

At the beginning of an allowed call, the agent must state:

- the company or legal entity responsible for the call;
- that the caller is an AI assistant or automated calling system;
- the real purpose of the call; and
- a simple way to stop future calls.

## Draft wording

> “Hi, I’m an AI assistant calling on behalf of **{company name}**. I’m calling to ask how you handle customer calls when your team is busy or closed, and to see whether our AI receptionist may help. Is now a reasonable time for one quick question?”

## Rules

Do not say “I’m a human,” “I’m returning your call,” or anything that conceals the system’s nature. Do not use this wording until the consent gate, campaign approval, caller identity, and jurisdiction-specific notice requirements are approved. If recording or transcription is enabled, use the separately approved recording notice.
